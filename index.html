How to Play

  • Steer: hold LEFT / RIGHT arrow keys, A / D, or the on-screen arrows. Tilt-free rolling!
  • Jump: SPACE, UP / W, or the JUMP button. Jump gaps and hop over spikes.
  • Goal: reach the checkered flag at the end of each track.
  • Danger: falling off the track or touching spikes / red movers costs 1 life. 3 lives per run.
  • Coins: grab gold coins. Coins unlock new balls and count toward your score.
  • Ramps: sloped tracks speed you up downhill — and slow you uphill. Time your jumps.
  • Pause: P key or the Pause button. Mute: M key or the Sound button.
